# User Story: Read chapter

As a **Bible researcher**\
I want to switch between the previous and the next chapter\
In order to navigate quickly

## Acceptance Criteria

### R1: user can go to the next chapter within a book

E: should go to the chapter 2 from chapter 1 of Genesis by next chapter

E: should go to the psalm 100 from psalm 99 by using by next chapter

E: should not change book when switching from chapter 5 to 6 in the Gospel of Mark

### R2: when user reaches the last chapter of the book it switches to the next book

E: should go to the psalm 100 from psalm 99 by using by next chapter

### R3: when user reaches the last chapter of the last book of the Bible it switches to the first book

E: should go to the next chapter of a book in the middle of the Bible

E: should go to the next chapter and not the last chapter of the last book of the Bible

E: should disable next chapter when reading the last chapter of the last book of the Bible
